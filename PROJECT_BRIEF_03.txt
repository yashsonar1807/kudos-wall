Internal Team Feedback & Peer Kudos Wall
🔒  Universal Security & Architecture Requirements
Pair Token Auth: Short-lived JWT Access Token (15m) + Long-lived Refresh Token (7d) in httpOnly
cookies.
Auth Features: Signup with Email Verification simulation, Login with token rotation, Forgot/Reset
Password.
UI Standard: Build all interface components using coss.com/ui primitives.
1. Overview & Goal
Create a company recognition platform where team members receive a monthly allowance of points to celebrate
peers alongside core company values, featuring a social feed and live monthly leaderboards.
2. Technical Feature Specifications
A. User Profiles & Recognition Wallets
User Schema: name , email , avatar , department , givingAllowance  (default: 100 pts/month), and
earnedPoints  (default: 0).
Profile page showing individual kudos history (received vs. sent) and earned recognition badges.
B. "Give Kudos" Workflow & Point Transactions
Modal with user autocomplete, point selector (10, 20, 50 pts), message field, and company value tag picker
(#Teamwork, #CustomerObsession, #Innovation).
Transaction Safety: Backend check ensuring sender has enough givingAllowance , prevention of self-gifting,
and atomic point transfer.
C. Social Kudos Feed
Company feed displaying rich cards with sender/receiver avatars, point badges, and value tags.
Feed pagination and infinite scrolling support.
Emoji reactions (+1, 👏 , 🔥 ) with optimistic UI updates.
D. Aggregated Leaderboard & Department Analytics
MongoDB aggregation pipelines ( $group , $sort , $limit ) ranking top-recognized employees of the month.
Filter rankings by department (Engineering, Design, Marketing, Sales).
Monthly allowance reset logic to refresh all users' givingAllowance  to 100 points.
